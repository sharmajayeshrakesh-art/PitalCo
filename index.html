The Heritage

A young house rooted in an ancient craft.

Brass — pittal — has warmed Indian homes and temples for over four thousand years. PittalCo carries that lineage forward: we work with the same hands, the same fire and the same reverence, but we design for the way you live now.

Every object begins as raw metal and ends as something quietly alive — a surface that catches candlelight, deepens with age, and belongs to a family longer than it belonged to us.

  • OriginCast and finished in the brass towns of India, where the craft has passed through generations.
  • MaterialSolid, unplated brass — no veneers, no shortcuts. Weight you can feel in the hand.
  • IntentMade slowly, in small batches, to be used daily and kept for a lifetime.

The Craft

Five hands,
one flame.

From molten alloy to mirror finish — the journey of a single PittalCo piece, told in five deliberate steps.

01 — The Alloy

The Alloy

We begin with pure copper and zinc, blended to the exact ratio that gives brass its golden warmth and lasting strength.

02 — The Cast

The Cast

Molten brass, glowing near a thousand degrees, is poured into hand-made moulds and left to find its first form.

03 — The Hammer

The Hammer

Artisans shape each vessel by hand, thousands of measured strikes drawing out the silhouette and its texture.

04 — The Polish

The Polish

Layer by layer, the surface is worked to a deep, reflective glow — the moment brass becomes light made solid.

05 — The Finish

The Finish

A protective hand-applied seal locks in the shine, then the maker signs the base. No two are ever alike.
